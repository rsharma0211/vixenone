4. REQUIREMENT FINDINGS (`requirement_findings`):
   - Return exactly one finding for every requirement `code` listed in REQUIREMENTS DATA.
   - `status` must be one of:
     * `compliant`: The proposal explicitly and unconditionally commits to meeting all elements of the requirement, with no conflicting clause elsewhere in the proposal.
     * `partially_compliant`: The proposal covers only part of the requirement or adds conditions/limitations (e.g., supervisor only during the day shift when 24x7 is required; washroom consumables included only "up to a reasonable usage level"; pest control arranged "on request" with "charges as applicable").
     * `non_compliant`: The proposal explicitly conflicts with or excludes the requirement (e.g., washroom consumables are "billed at actuals" when required in the monthly fee; deep cleaning is "not included"; working days state Monday to Friday with weekend services at additional cost when Monday to Saturday is required).
     * `not_evidenced`: The proposal omits the requirement or uses vague, unquantified statements that do not verify compliance (e.g., "adequate manpower as required" without stating headcount; "requests are attended to promptly" without stating a 2-hour response SLA).
   - `is_inference`: Set `false` when directly supported by a quote; set `true` when `not_evidenced` or inferred from omission/context.
   - `confidence`: `high`, `medium`, or `low`.
   - `risk_types`: List any applicable risks from `["exclusion", "omission", "ambiguity", "contradiction", "unknown_cost"]`:
     * `exclusion`: Vendor excludes a required item or service (e.g. deep cleaning not included, consumables excluded from base).
     * `unknown_cost`: Vendor states an item is billed "at actuals", "charges as applicable", "at additional cost" without a fixed monthly price, or subject to an undefined "reasonable usage level".
     * `ambiguity`: Vendor uses vague wording instead of a concrete commitment (e.g. "adequate manpower", "attended to promptly", "reasonable usage level").
     * `contradiction`: Two parts of the proposal conflict (e.g. Section 1 says Monday to Saturday, while Section 6 says Monday to Friday and weekend services at additional cost).
     * `omission`: Proposal fails to address a required item or SLA.
   - `severity`: Set `high`, `medium`, or `low` whenever `risk_types` is non-empty (use `high` for mandatory exclusions, contradictions, or unquantified mandatory costs; `medium` or `low` for minor/non-mandatory gaps), or `null` when `risk_types` is empty.
   - `clarification_question`: Provide a specific, actionable question for the vendor whenever `status` is not `compliant` or `risk_types` is non-empty; otherwise `null`.
5. JUDGED CRITERION FINDINGS (`criterion_findings`):

     * `partially_compliant` if the proposal covers some elements but has gaps, exclusions, or vague commitments.
     * `non_compliant` or `not_evidenced` if essential elements of the criterion are missing or contradicted (e.g. no SLA response time or penalties stated).
   - Include a verbatim contiguous `quote` from the proposal (if applicable), `page`, `section`, and a concise `rationale`.
6. STANDALONE RISKS (`risks`):
   - Only include cross-cutting proposal risks not already captured on a specific requirement finding (leave empty `[]` if all risks are already attached to `requirement_findings`).
7. COST BREAKDOWN (`cost`):

   - `included`: Items explicitly included in the monthly base fee (`{item, quote, page}`).
   - `excluded`: Items explicitly excluded from the monthly base fee (`{item, quote, page}`).
   - `optional`: Items offered as optional add-ons with a stated price (`{item, quote, page}`).
   - `unknown`: Any items with unquantified or variable charges such as "billed at actuals", "charges as applicable", "at additional cost", or excess over "reasonable usage level" (`{item, quote, page}`). If the vendor states "No other charges apply" and has no unquantified items, `unknown` must be `[]`.
